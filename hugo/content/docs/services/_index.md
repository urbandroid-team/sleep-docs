# Connected Services

**Sleep as Android** can integrate with a wide variety of third-party platforms and services to sync your health data, automate your smart home, and enhance your overall sleep experience.

Use this section to explore how to connect the app with your favorite ecosystem.

## Data Safety
Keep your sleep history secure and synchronized across all your devices.

*   [**Backup Data**](/docs/services/backup_data): Learn how to manually export data or use cloud storage.
*   [**SleepCloud**](/docs/services/sleepcloud): Our dedicated service for seamless data synchronization and web-based analysis.


## Health & Fitness Platforms
Sync your sleep duration, heart rate, and other vitals with major health tracking services.

*   [**Google Fit**](/docs/services/google_fit): Upload sleep data and use activity targets to influence your sleep tags.
*   [**Health Connect**](/docs/services/health_connect): The new Android standard for sharing health data between apps securely.
*   [**Samsung Health**](/docs/services/s_health): Full integration for Samsung users to sync sleep and activity data.


## Automation & Smart Home
Trigger lights, coffee machines, or custom scripts based on your sleep state (e.g., when tracking starts or when an alarm triggers).

*   [**Automation Overview**](/docs/services/automation): A guide to the available events and triggers.
*   [**IFTTT**](/docs/services/ifttt): Connect with hundreds of external IoT devices and web services.
*   [**MQTT**](/docs/services/mqtt): Publish real-time sleep events to your local smart home broker (like Home Assistant).
*   [**Custom Webhooks**](/docs/services/custom_webhooks): POST sleep events to any custom URL or API endpoint.
*   [**Tasker & Automate**](/docs/services/tasker_automate): Use powerful Android-native automation apps for deep device control.
    *   [Tasker Walkthroughs](/docs/services/tasker_walkthroughs) | [Automate Walkthroughs](/docs/services/automate_walkthroughs)
*   **Google_home**: Connect with BETA Google Home (limited access to 100 users per one app).


## Productivity & Lifestyle
Enhance your daily routine by connecting your calendar, voice assistant, or music streaming.

*   [**Google Calendar**](/docs/services/google_calendar): Automatically skip alarms on holidays or reschedule them based on upcoming events.
*   [**Google Assistant (App Actions)**](/docs/services/google_app_actions): Control sleep tracking and alarms using voice commands.
*   [**Spotify**](/docs/services/spotify): Wake up to your favorite playlists or fall asleep to calming soundscapes.
