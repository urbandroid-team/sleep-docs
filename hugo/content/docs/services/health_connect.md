# Health Connect


This guide will help you connect **Sleep as Android** with Google's **Health Connect** platform. By enabling this integration, you can easily centralize your health data and keep your favorite fitness, wellness, and sleep ecosystem fully synchronized.

> 🚀 **Beta Feature Note:** The Health Connect service is currently in **BETA**, we are waiting for Google to release the stable version.

---

## What It Does

Integrating with Health Connect enables a two-way flow of information between Sleep as Android and your other health apps:

* **Export:** Automatically sync your detailed sleep phases from Sleep as Android to Health Connect.
* **Import:** Seamlessly bring 3rd-party sleep tracking data into Sleep as Android.
* **Heart Rate Sync:** Automatically exchange vital stats like resting heart rate (HR).
* **Oxygen Levels:** Securely exchange blood oxygen saturation (SpO<sub>2</sub>) data.


## Where to Find It

To set up the integration, open the **Sleep as Android** app on your device and navigate to:
`Settings` ➔ `Services` ➔ `Health Connect`

## Setup Guide

Connecting your data takes just a few quick steps:

1. **Enable the Feature:** Open the app, go to `Settings` ➔ `Services` ➔ `Health Connect`, and toggle the switch to turn it on.
2. **Grant Permissions:** A system window will appear asking for access permissions. Be sure to grant the necessary read/write permissions to Sleep as Android so it can exchange data.
3. **Sync Your Data:** Tap the **Sync** button on the settings screen to trigger your very first manual data synchronization.
4. **All Set!** Once completed, you can safely return to your main settings. Health Connect is now active and will work in the background.
