# For Developers

**Sleep as Android** is built to be open and extensible. We provide a variety of APIs and documentation for power users and third-party developers to automate sleep tasks, integrate new hardware, or access sleep data.

---

## Automation & Integration
Use standard Android mechanisms to control the app or react to sleep events.

*   [**Intent API**](/docs/devs/intent_api): Broadcast actions and receive real-time sleep events (e.g., tracking started, REM phase detected).
*   [**Content Provider API**](/docs/devs/content_provider_api): Read sleep data or manage alarms directly from your own app.

---

## Hardware & Custom Tasks
Extend the app's functionality with your own hardware or puzzles.

*   [**Wearable API**](/docs/devs/wearable_api): Integrate custom tracking devices or smartwatches.
*   [**CAPTCHA API**](/docs/devs/captcha_api): Create your own custom wake-up tasks and puzzles.

---

## Data & Cloud
Understand our data formats or integrate with our cloud service.

*   [**CSV Documentation**](/docs/devs/csv): A detailed breakdown of the fields in our standard data export format.
*   [**SleepCloud Storage API**](/docs/devs/sleepcloud_api): Fetch user sleep records from the cloud using OAuth2 authentication.
