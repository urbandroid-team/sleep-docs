# Alarms

**Sleep as Android** provides a highly customizable alarm system designed to help you wake up refreshed and ensure you never oversleep.

From our signature **Smart Wake-up** that catches you in light sleep, to brain-teasing **CAPTCHAs** that ensure you're truly awake, use this section to build your perfect morning routine.

---

## The Basics
Get started with creating and configuring your alarms.

*   [**Create Alarm**](/docs/alarms/create_alarm): A guide to setting your first alarm or nap.
*   [**Alarm Settings**](/docs/alarms/alarm_settings): Explore default and per-alarm configuration options.

---

## Wake-up Features
Tools to help you transition from sleep to wakefulness.

*   [**Smart Wake-up**](/docs/sleep/smart_wake_up): Learn how the app finds the ideal moment to wake you up.
*   [**Snooze**](/docs/alarms/snooze): Configure snooze durations, limits, and the "halve-the-snooze" feature.
*   [**CAPTCHA**](/docs/alarms/captcha): Mini-tasks (math, QR codes, etc.) you must solve to turn off the alarm.
*   [**Backup Alarm**](/docs/alarms/backup): A fail-safe loud alarm that triggers if you ignore your primary wake-up call.

---

## Sound & Environment
Customize how your alarm sounds and how it interacts with your room.

*   [**Sound & Ringtones**](/docs/alarms/ringtone): Choose nature sounds, local music, Spotify, or online radio.
*   [**Smartlight**](/docs/devices/smart_light): Integrate sunrise-like wake-up lights with your alarm.

## Planning & Specialized Sleeping
Stay on track with your sleep goals and unique schedules.

*   [**Bedtime Notification**](/docs/alarms/bedtime_notification): Friendly reminders to head to bed on time based on your sleep goal.
*   [**Naps**](/docs/alarms/naps): Quick timers for power naps and restorative rest.
*   [**Power Nap**](/docs/alarms/power_nap): A specialized smart alarm for flexible wake-up times.
*   [**Polyphasic Sleeping**](/docs/alarms/polyphasic): Setup guides for complex multi-nap sleep schedules.
