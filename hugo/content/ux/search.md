**Finds you anything inside Sleep as Android. Settings, documentation, alarms, sleep records...**

The global search tool is your fastest way to find anything inside Sleep as Android. Located right in the top bar of almost every screen, it lets you quickly track down settings, documentation, alarms, history, and more.

Because Sleep as Android offers a deep and comprehensive set of features, using the search bar is often the quickest shortcut to get things done.

Search will search through:

| | What can it find | What can you search by | Example query |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Settings** | Anything inside the settings - tapping takes you to the specific settings screen and emphasizes the searched option | <ul><li>Option name</li><li>Option description</li><li>Various aliases</li></ul> Just try what comes to your mind. | **eng** - find the "Force English" option to switch language |
| **Documentation** | Looks through this documentation | Documentation chapter names | **search** - finds you this page |
| **Alarm** | Looks through active and inactive alarms | Alarm time | **2:50** - finds you alarms that occur at 2:50 |
| **Add-ons** | Looks through add-ons for Sleep as Android | Add-on name | **cap** - finds the Captcha pack add-on |
| **Graphs** | Looks through your sleep records | <ul><li>Sleep record date</li><li>Time</li><li>Comment</li><li>Tags</li></ul> | **watch** - finds all records that have been created with a smartwatch |
| **Noise** | Looks through your sleep noise recordings | <ul><li>Date</li><li>Comment</li><li>Tags</li></ul> | **talk** - finds all recordings with #talk detected |
