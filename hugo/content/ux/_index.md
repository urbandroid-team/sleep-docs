# Personalize & App Layout

**Sleep as Android** offers a highly flexible interface that can be tailored to match your personal preferences and daily workflow.

Use this chapter to explore the different home screen layouts, learn how to navigate the app, and discover every customization option available in your app settings.

---

## App Settings Breakdown

Looking for a specific toggle or option in your app settings?

*   [**Personalization Settings**](/ux/personalize): An item-by-item guide explaining every option found in `Settings` ➔ `Personalize` inside the app, including themes, graph color palettes, morning screen tiles, time & date formats, text-to-speech, and analog time pickers.

---

## Home Screen Layouts

Sleep as Android lets you choose between three distinct home screen layouts to fit your style:

*   [**Dashboard Layout**](/ux/hs_dashboard): A modern, card-based overview showing your next alarms, sleep score, and quick action cards.
*   [**Tabs Layout**](/ux/hs_tabs): A classic tabbed interface with separate, swipeable sections for Alarms, Graphs, and Stats.
*   [**Alarms Only Mode**](/ux/hs_alarms_only): A minimalist, distraction-free alarm clock interface that hides all tracking features.

---

## Navigation & Tools

Master navigating the app and accessing your favorite features quickly:

*   [**Left ☰ Menu**](/ux/left_menu): Your central navigation drawer for accessing support, settings, noise libraries, and app features.
*   [**Home Screen Widgets**](/ux/widgets): Add tracking controls, alarm lists, and nap shortcuts directly to your phone's home screen.
*   [**In-App Search**](/ux/search): Instantly find any setting, documentation page, or historical sleep record.
