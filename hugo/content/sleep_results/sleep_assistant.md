# Sleep Assistant 🤖💬


Ever looked at your morning sleep graph and wondered, *"Okay... but what does all of this actually mean for my day?"* You're not alone!

The **Sleep Assistant** is your friendly, personal sleep interpreter. It takes all the raw numbers, hypnograms, and night statistics that the app collects and translates them into simple, warm, easy-to-understand text summaries and practical tips.

> [!NOTE]
> **Full Version Feature:** Sleep Assistant is available exclusively in the **[Full (paid) version](/getting_started/plan.html)** of Sleep as Android.

---

## How Sleep Assistant Helps You

Sleep tracking gathers thousands of tiny data points while you rest - from deep sleep cycles and awakenings to heart rate dips and snoring. While charts and graphs are wonderful for visual detail, reading them when you're half-awake in the morning isn't always easy!

The **Sleep Assistant** is here to make your mornings effortless:

* 💬 **Plain-Language Explanations:** Turns complicated sensor charts into clear, friendly stories you can read in a few seconds.
* 🔍 **Personal Context:** Compares your night against your own recent sleep baseline, letting you know what went great and what changed.
* 💡 **Practical Guidance:** Can offer gentle, realistic suggestions to help you wind down better and wake up feeling refreshed.

---

## Where to Find Sleep Assistant in the App

You can ask the Sleep Assistant for insights whenever you're curious:

1. **On Your Morning Briefing:** Right after you stop sleep tracking, scroll down your morning summary screen to find the **Sleep Assistant** card for your fresh daily breakdown. *(The Assistant automatically uses the **Today** topic as the default question on the Morning Screen!)*
2. **On Any Saved Sleep Graph:** Open any past sleep graph from your history and tap the **Assistant** icon to get an instant explanation of that night's rest.
3. **In the Main Menu ☰:** Open the `Left ☰ Menu` ➔ `Sleep Assistant` anytime to catch up on your recent sleep trends.

## How the Sleep Assistant Works

Getting insights from the Sleep Assistant is simple, friendly, and structured in two easy steps:

### Step 1: Pick a Starter Topic

Whenever you open the Assistant, choose whichever starter question fits what you're curious about today:

| Topic | Question Prompt | What It Tells You |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Briefly** | *"Summarize my sleep quality"* | Gives you a quick summary of your overall sleep qualities. |
| **Today** <br>*(Default on Morning Screen)* | *"How did I sleep today?"* | Gives you a detailed morning breakdown of your latest sleep depth, awakenings, and rest. This is the default question automatically selected when opening the Assistant card on your Morning Briefing! |
| **Sleep Score** | *"Explain my Sleep Score"* | Breaks down your sleep score pillars (duration, deep sleep %, efficiency, regularity, and heart rate) in plain language. |
| **Community** | *"How do I sleep compared to others?"* | Compares your average sleep stats against general benchmarks. |

---

### Step 2: Ask a Follow-up Question
Once the Assistant answers your starter question, you can type **one follow-up question** if you'd like to explore a topic a little deeper!

> 💡 **Fun Follow-up Ideas:**
> * *"How can I lower my snoring?"*
> * *"What is a good, relaxing bedtime routine?"*
> * *"Why was my deep sleep a bit lower?"*

## Privacy & Data Security

Your trust and privacy mean everything to us. Sleep Assistant is designed from the ground up to keep your personal life private:

* **Active User Initiation Only:** The Sleep Assistant is completely passive. It never runs automatically in the background or processes anything until you tap a question.
* **No Personal Data Shared:** The app never collects, shares, or transmits personal identifying details.
* **Aggregated Sleep Metrics Only:** Only anonymous, non-personal numerical totals (such as total sleep hours, deep sleep percentages, and average heart rate) are used to craft your text summaries.
* **Stored on Your Device:** Your sleep records and history remain safely stored locally on your phone.

**No.** Sleep Assistant is a friendly educational tool designed for wellness, motivation, and daily sleep hygiene. It does not provide medical diagnoses or medical treatment. If you suspect a sleep disorder (such as sleep apnea), please consult a healthcare professional.
