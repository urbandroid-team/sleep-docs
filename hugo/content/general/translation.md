Sleep as Android is translated into many world languages, thanks to our incredible community of volunteer translators. We could never achieve this without your help!

## Can you help us with translations?

As we add new features, we frequently need help translating new text strings. If you'd like to contribute, please request access to our translation spreadsheets below:

*   [**Sleep as Android** Translation Spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1N3CrEP19aB66DBFzONIPooH5dWUW_mU4ENH_MeD1QFU/edit)
*   [**SleepCloud** Translation Spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1Slw1_Z2tN_berThYKivW6KOCNbW1DY-TUWkwclRvnJU/edit)
*   [**BabySleep** Translation Spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1UCeO0d2_rfjKJLdkPygmEcmnHcLTw9VgdXNHuIpPkeY/edit)
*   [**Twilight** Translation Spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1Jse7DqJr4eNTca6_7rSBchpaX3eU6SgLo9V3ltBio5Q/edit)
*   [**Digital Detox** Translation Spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1jgE_VaECnMzUmg0apblRLVqg59abJhj30ylZUSF9zb8/edit)

We use [**Translate the Sheet**](https://tts.urbandroid.org/index.html) to streamline the process for our volunteers.
