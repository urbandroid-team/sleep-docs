# Alarms

**Sleep as Android** provides a highly customizable alarm system designed to help you wake up refreshed and ensure you never oversleep.

Use this chapter to learn how to create alarms, customize alarm settings, and configure smart wake-ups, CAPTCHAs, ringtones, and bedtime reminders.

---

## App Settings & Options Breakdown

Looking for a specific toggle or option in your alarm settings?

*   [**Alarm Settings Breakdown**](/alarms/alarm_settings.html): An item-by-item guide explaining global defaults (`Settings` ➔ `Alarms`) and per-alarm overrides—including gentle volume ramp-ups, sound delays, backup alarms, orientation locks, fullscreen alarms, and button reassignments.

## The Basics & Creation

Get started with setting up your wake-up routine:

*   [**Create Alarm**](/alarms/create_alarm.html): A step-by-step guide to setting your first daily alarm, nap, or one-time wake-up timer.

## Wake-up Features

Tools to help you transition smoothly from sleep to wakefulness and ensure you stay awake:

*   [**Smart Wake-up**](/sleep/smart_wake_up.html): Learn how the app detects light sleep and wakes you up at the optimal moment.
*   [**Snooze**](/alarms/snooze.html): Configure snooze durations, maximum snooze caps, and the "halve-the-snooze" option.
*   [**CAPTCHA Tasks**](/alarms/captcha.html): Require solving math puzzles, scanning QR codes/barcodes, or physical tasks before turning off alarms.
*   [**Backup Alarm**](/alarms/backup.html): A fail-safe max-volume alarm that triggers if your primary alarm is left unhandled.
