# Naps


Need a quick power-up or a short afternoon rest? The **Naps** feature lets you set a timer-based alarm that rings after a specific duration (e.g., in 20 minutes) rather than at a specific time of day (e.g., at 2:15 PM).


## How Naps Differ from Regular Alarms

* **Regular Alarm:** Scheduled for a fixed time on the clock (e.g., wake up at 7:00 AM).
* **Nap Alarm:** Counts down from the moment you set it (e.g., wake up in 15 minutes).

Nap durations can be customized anywhere from **1 minute up to 10 hours**.

## How to Create a Nap Alarm

1. Open **Sleep as Android** and go to the Alarms screen.
2. Tap the **(+) Add alarm** button.
3. On the time picker, switch to **Nap** mode.
3. Choose your desired nap duration from the list (or set a custom length).
4. Save the nap to start the countdown!

## Managing Nap Settings

* **Automatic Cleanup:** By default, nap alarms automatically delete themselves after they ring and are dismissed. This keeps your main alarm list clean and clutter-free!
* **Reusing Nap Alarms:** If you take the same quick nap regularly and want to keep it saved, you can turn off automatic deletion:
  * Open the specific nap's settings.
  * Uncheck **Delete after ringing**.
