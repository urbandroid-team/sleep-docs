We all have those mornings when we just need a few extra minutes in bed. Snoozing lets you temporarily delay your wake-up, but to make sure you don't accidentally oversleep, we also offer a variety of helpful limits.

## Where to Configure Snoozes

* **Global Default:** Set your baseline snooze duration and limits for all new alarms by navigating to `Settings` → `Alarms` → `Snooze`.
* **Individual Alarms:** Override the default snooze behavior for a specific alarm in its `Per-alarm settings` → `Snooze`.

> **Example:**
> If your alarm goes off at 5:30 AM, and you wake up after a while at 5:33 AM but decide you need a bit more rest, you can hit the **10-minute snooze** button. Your alarm will pause and gently wake you again at 5:43 AM (5:33 + 10 minutes).

## Your Snooze Options

You can customize your overall snooze preferences by navigating to `Settings` → `Alarms` → `Snooze`. You can also tweak these for individual alarms under **Alarm-specific settings**.

Here is a breakdown of the tools you can use to build your perfect wake-up routine:

### Snooze Duration
Set your go-to snooze length. This is the default time that appears on the large Snooze button when your alarm rings.

> [!TIP]
> If you want more options right on the alarm screen, turn on **Changing snooze time** below!

### Maximum Total Snooze Time
A great safety net for heavy sleepers! This setting limits the *total* amount of time you are allowed to snooze. Once you've snoozed for this total duration, the Snooze button will disappear, leaving only the **Dismiss** option. Time to get up!

### Snooze Limit
Rather than limiting the total time, this setting caps the *number of times* you can hit snooze. Once you've used up your allowed snoozes, the green Snooze button goes away, and you must dismiss the alarm.

> [!TIP]
> You can combine both **Snooze limit** and **Maximum total snooze time** to keep your morning routine on track! Whichever limit is reached first will automatically disable the Snooze button.

### Halve the Snooze
Want to gently ease yourself awake? When enabled, each consecutive snooze gets cut in half.

> **Example:**
> If your starting snooze time is 10 minutes, hitting snooze again will give you 5 minutes, then 3 minutes, and so on. It’s a great way to slowly transition out of sleep!

### Changing Snooze Time
Need flexibility in the morning? Enabling this adds a selection of different snooze durations to your alarm screen, right alongside your default time. You can pick exactly how much extra sleep you need in the moment.

> [!TIP]
> If your alarm goes off during the **Smart period**, one of the available snooze options will dynamically show the exact time remaining until your original scheduled alarm time!
>
> **Example:** Your alarm is set for 7:00 AM, but Smart Wake Up wakes you at 6:42 AM. On the alarm screen, you will see a quick snooze option for **18 minutes**, allowing you to snooze right up until your target 7:00 AM time.

### Snooze After Alarm Time
This setting works hand-in-hand with our **Smart Wake Up** feature. It completely disables snoozing once your originally scheduled alarm time is reached.

> **Example:**
> If your alarm is set for 7:00 AM, but Smart Wake Up catches you in light sleep and rings at 6:47 AM, you can only snooze for a total of 13 minutes. Once the clock strikes 7:00 AM, snoozing is disabled!


## ❓ FAQ & Troubleshooting

<details>
<summary><strong>Why can't I see the Snooze button when my alarm rings?</strong></summary>

*   **You reached your snooze limit:** Check if **Snooze limit** or **Maximum total snooze time** is enabled. Once these thresholds are met, the Snooze button is hidden to prevent oversleeping.
*   **Snooze after alarm time is disabled:** If you are past your scheduled alarm time and **Snooze after alarm time** is disabled, snoozing is disabled.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Why does the snooze time on the alarm screen not match my set snooze duration?</strong></summary>

*   **Per-alarm settings overriding global settings:** You might have customized snooze options for that specific alarm. Check the individual **Alarm-specific settings** to ensure it isn't overriding your global preferences (`Settings` → `Alarms` → `Snooze`).
*   **Maximum total snooze time cap:** If your remaining allowed total snooze time is shorter than your default snooze duration, the button will adjust to fit only the time you have left.
*   **Snooze limit configuration:** If your **Snooze limit** or **Maximum total snooze time** is set lower than expected, consecutive snoozes may be shortened or cut off early to prevent you from exceeding your limits.
*   **"Halve the Snooze" is active:** Check if **Halve the snooze** is enabled. This feature cuts each consecutive snooze duration in half (e.g., 20 min → 10 min → 5 min...).
</details>
