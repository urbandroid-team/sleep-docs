# Editing graphs

**Any sleep graph can be further edited. You can rate the sleep, add additional awake periods, cut excess time, and add tags or comments.**

**On any sleep graph, tap the pencil ![ic_pencil](/assets/icons/ic_pencil.svg) icon.**

---

## Rating Sleep

Rate your sleep on the morning screen by tapping on the stars.

*   **0** = worst sleep
*   **5** = best sleep

<div style="text-align: center;">
  <img src="/docs/assets/images/edit_1.png" alt="Rating sleep" style="max-width: 400px; width: 100%;" />
</div>

> [!NOTE]
> If you leave the rating untouched, it won't count towards your statistics. If you touch it and drag it to zero, it will count as 0.

## Adding Comments and Tags

Choose any of the available icon tags from the list. With the **(+)** button you can also create your own personal tags (`#any_text` or `#emoji`) — read more in [Tags](/sleep/tags).

Tap on the **Comment** section to type anything you would like to save as a note.

## Adding Awakes and Trimming the Graph

1.  From the **Morning Screen**, tap on the **Hypnogram** to enter the **Edit mode**.
2.  **Drag across** the graph section that you wish to edit.
3.  That section will **zoom in** for more precise selection using the handles.
4.  For fine-grained selection, you can adjust the time manually in the upper bar.
5.  When the period is selected, tap on the **scissors icon** in the upper right corner.

*   **Trimming:** If at least one end of the selected period touches the start or end of the graph, the whole period is cut off (the graph is shortened).
*   **Adding Awake:** When the selected period is within the graph, the period is transformed into an **awake period**, and its duration is subtracted from the total sleep duration.

<div style="text-align: center;">
  <img src="/docs/assets/images/edit_3.png" alt="Trimming and adding awakes" style="max-width: 400px; width: 100%;" />
</div>

> [!NOTE]
> If you are missing an entire sleep record, you can add it manually. Go to **Left ☰ Menu → Graphs** or the [**Statistics**](/sleep/statistics) screen and use the **(+) Add sleep** ![plus](/assets/icons/plus.svg) button.

---

## Deleting Awakes

1.  **Drag across** the awake period on the graph that you wish to remove.
2.  The section will zoom in.
3.  Tap on the **eye icon** in the top right corner.
4.  Choose **Delete Awake**.

> [!TIP]
> You can delete all awakes at once by tapping the **(⁝) Menu** button in the top right corner and choosing **Delete Awake**.

> [!WARNING]
> Deleting awakes will not work for **paused tracking** (as there is no sensor data for that period) or for awakes that were manually entered by the user.
