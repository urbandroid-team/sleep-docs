# Editing graphs

**Any sleep graph can be further edited. You can rate the sleep, add additional awake periods, cut excess time, and add tags or comments.**

_On any sleep graph, tap the pencil ![ic_pencil](/assets/icons/ic_pencil.svg) icon._

---

## Rating sleep

| Description | Preview |
| :--- | :---: |
| Rate your sleep on the morning screen by tapping on the stars.<br><br>0 = worst sleep, 5 = best sleep.<br><br>> **Note:** If you leave the rating untouched, it won't count towards your statistics. If you touch it and drag it to zero, it will count as 0. | ![Rate sleep](/assets/images/edit_1.png) |

## Adding awakes, editing start / end of the tracking

| Step-by-Step Guide | Preview |
| :--- | :---: |
| 1. From the Morning screen, tap on the Hypnogram to enter Edit mode.<br>2. Drag across the graph section that you wish to edit.<br>3. That section will zoom in for more precise selection with the handles.<br>4. For fine-grained selection, you can adjust the time manually in the upper bar.<br>5. When the period is selected, tap on the **scissors icon** in the upper right corner:<br><ul><li>If at least one end of the selected period is touching the start or end of the graph, the whole period is cut off (the graph is shortened).</li><li>When the selected period is within the graph, the period is transformed into an awake period, and its duration is subtracted from total sleep duration.</li></ul> | ![Edit graph awakes](/assets/images/edit_3.png) |

> **Note:** If you are missing any sleep time (you want to add time rather than subtract), you can add your missing time as a new record. To do this, go to the list of Graphs (_Left ☰ menu_ &rarr; _Graphs_) or to the [Stats](/sleep/statistics) screen and use ![plus](/assets/icons/plus.svg) **Add sleep**.

> **Note:** Deleting awakes won't work for paused tracking (there is no sensor data from that period) or for awakes manually entered by the user.
