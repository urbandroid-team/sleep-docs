# Connected Devices

**Sleep as Android** supports a wide range of external hardware to improve the accuracy of sleep tracking and enhance your wake-up experience.

From smartwatches that track your heart rate to bedside lamps that simulate a natural sunrise, use this section to set up and configure your devices.

---

## Wearables (Smartwatches & Trackers)
The most common way to track sleep movement and heart rate. We support almost every major platform.

*   [**Wearables Overview**](/devices/wearables): How to get started with any watch.
*   [**Supported Devices List**](/devices/supported_wearable): Check if your specific model is integrated.
*   **Platform Guides:**
    *   [**Wear OS**](/devices/wearos) (Pixel Watch, TicWatch, Fossil, etc.)
    *   [**One UI Watch**](/devices/one_ui) (Samsung Galaxy Watch 4, 5, 6, 7)
    *   [**Garmin**](/devices/garmin)

    *   [**Pebble**](/devices/pebble)

## Specialized Sleep Hardware
Dedicated tools designed for high-precision sleep monitoring.

*   [**Sleep Phaser**](/devices/sleep_phaser): A contactless bedside lamp with an integrated PIR sensor for tracking without wearing anything.
    *   [Version 2 & 3](/devices/sleep_phaser_2) | [Version 1](/devices/sleep_phaser_1)
*   [**Sleep Mask**](/devices/sleep_mask): Light-stimulated mask for better wake-ups and lucid dreaming.
*   [**Oximeter**](/devices/oximeter): Standalone Bluetooth sensors for continuous SpO<sub>2</sub> (oxygen) monitoring.

---

## Environment & Smart Home
Control your bedroom environment based on your sleep state.

*   [**Smartlight**](/devices/smart_light): Integrate Philips Hue, IKEA Trådfri, or your phone's screen for sunrise alarms and "night-light" functionality.
