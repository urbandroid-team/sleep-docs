# Supported wearables - list

**Sleep as Android** supports a wide range of smartwatches and fitness trackers. Whether you use a modern Wear OS device, a classic Pebble, or a high-precision Polar strap, you can monitor your sleep cycles with ease.

### 🏆 Our Top Pick

**TicWatch 5 PRO:** We currently recommend this model for the best experience. It offers a complete sensor suite including SpO2, HR, and HRV. [Read more about our top pick here](https://sleep.urbandroid.org/we-finally-have-a-new-king-of-smart-watch/).

**Pebble Time 2:** As long-time fans of the original, we are thrilled to support the new Pebble models as well.

---

### Legend for Sensors
*   **↝** : Movement (detected via accelerometer)
*   **❤** : Heart Rate (HR)
*   **💟** : Heart Rate Variability (HRV)
*   **◌** : Blood Oxygen Saturation (SpO2)
