# Supported wearables - list

**Sleep as Android** supports a wide range of smartwatches and fitness trackers. Whether you use a modern Wear OS device, a classic Pebble, or a high-precision Polar strap, you can monitor your sleep cycles with ease.

### 🏆 Our Top Pick
**TicWatch 5 PRO:** We currently recommend this model for the best experience. It offers a complete sensor suite including SpO<sub>2</sub>, HR, and HRV. [Read more about our top pick here](https://sleep.urbandroid.org/we-finally-have-a-new-king-of-smart-watch/).

---

### Legend for Sensors
*   **↝** : Movement (detected via accelerometer)
*   **❤** : Heart Rate (HR)
*   **💟** : Heart Rate Variability (HRV)
*   **◌** : Blood Oxygen Saturation (SpO<sub>2</sub>)

---

## Supported Devices

| Manufacturer | Device | Supported Sensors | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mobvoi** | TicWatch Pro 5, Pro 3, E3 | ↝ ❤ 💟 ◌ | **Full Support.** Includes continuous SpO<sub>2</sub> and HRV tracking. |
| **Wear OS** | Other Wear OS watches | ↝ ❤ | Requires the [watch app](/devices/wearos.html) from the Play Store. |
| **Pebble** | All models (Time, 2, Classic) | ↝ ❤ | [Pebble is still supported!](/devices/pebble.html) HR available on Pebble 2. |
| **Google** | Pixel Watch 1, 2, 3 | ↝ ❤ | SpO<sub>2</sub> and HRV data is currently restricted by Google for 3rd party apps. |
| **Polar** | H10, OH1, Vantage, Pacer, Ignite | ❤ 💟 | High-precision tracking via [Polar BT](https://sleep.urbandroid.org/polar-sleep-tracking) or [Polar SDK](https://github.com/polarofficial/polar-ble-sdk/blob/master/documentation/UsingSDKWithWatches.md). |
| **Samsung** | Galaxy Watch 4 through 9 | ↝ ❤ | Requires the [watch app](/devices/one_ui.html) from the Play Store. |
| **Xiaomi** | Mi Band 1 through 9 | ↝ ❤ | Connect via [Notify for Mi Band](/devices/notify.html) or [Tools & Mi Band](/devices/tools). |
| **Amazfit** | Bip, GTS, GTR, T-Rex (most models) | ↝ ❤ | Connect via [Notify for Amazfit](/devices/notify.html) or [Tools & Amazfit](/devices/tools). |
| **Huawei** | Watch 1, 2, 3, 4, GT series | ↝ ❤ | Connect via [Health Connect](/services/health_connect.html) or [Notify](/devices/notify.html). |
| **Garmin** | All Connect IQ 2.0+ models | ↝ ❤ 💟 ◌ | Requires the [watch app from Garmin IQ Store](/devices/garmin.html). |
| **Fitbit** | Versa, Sense, Ionic | ↝ ❤ | Requires the **Fitbit Add-on**. Limited support. |

---

> [!TIP]
> **Don't see your device?** Many other wearables can connect via standard Bluetooth Smart (BLE) protocols. Check the [Wearables Overview](/devices/wearables.html) for generic connection steps.
