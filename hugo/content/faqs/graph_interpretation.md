# Understanding your sleep graphs

### 1. What does a "healthy" sleep graph look like?
A healthy night of monophasic sleep typically looks like a **"rollercoaster"** across 7-8 hours.
*   It consists of repeating **90-minute sleep cycles**.
*   Cycles start with **Deep Sleep** (valleys at the base of the graph).
*   Cycles transition into **Light Sleep / REM** (peaks towards the top).
*   Over the course of the night, deep sleep periods usually get shorter, while REM periods become longer.

### 2. What do the colored lines on my graph mean?
*   **Red Line:** Heart Rate (HR) in beats per minute. Bubbles show your minimum/maximum HR.
*   **Violet Line:** Heart Rate Variability (HRV), indicating physical recovery.
*   **Blue Line:** Blood Oxygen (SpO2) measured via a pulse oximeter or supported watch.
*   **Turquoise Line:** Breath Rate (breaths per minute).
*   **Orange Line:** Ambient room light measured in LUX.
*   **Dashed Vertical Line:** Marks the start of your **Smart Period** window.

### 3. Why is one of my sensor lines missing?
Sensor lines (e.g., HR, HRV, or SpO2) only appear on the graph if:
1.  **Hardware Connection:** A compatible wearable or external device was actively connected during the entire tracking session.
2.  **Configuration:** The sensor is enabled in `Settings` ➔ `Sleep tracking` ➔ `Wearables`.
3.  **Breath Tracking:** Sonar or a wearable was active for breath rate tracking.
If your hardware is connected but data is missing, check the device's battery and Bluetooth connection stability.
