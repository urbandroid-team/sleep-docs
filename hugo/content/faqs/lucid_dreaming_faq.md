# Lucid Dreaming

### 1. Why are Lucid dreaming cues not working?
*   **Do Not Disturb (DND):** Ensure the app is in your DND exception list.
*   **Watch Settings:** Check that your watch isn't in a mode that blocks vibrations (Bedtime/Theater mode).
*   **Placement:** Ensure the phone's microphone isn't muffled by bedding.

### 2. Lucid cues keep waking me up completely
*   👉 **Fix:** Reduce the volume slider or lower the repetition frequency in `Settings` ➔ `Sleep tracking` ➔ `Lucid dreaming`. Try using wearable vibration instead of audio cues.

### 3. Phone and watch both vibrate during preview, but only watch at night
**This is intentional.** The preview tests all hardware. During actual tracking, vibrations are routed exclusively to your wearable to keep alerts quiet and effective.

### 4. The default ringtone does not work for me
*   👉 **Fix:** You can change the cue sound to something that works better for your subconscious. Go to `Settings` ➔ `Sleep tracking` ➔ `Lucid dreaming` ➔ `Sound`. You can choose from built-in sounds or even use your own media files from your phone's storage.
