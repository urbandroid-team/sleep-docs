# Automatic tracking issues

### 1. Why isn't automatic sleep tracking or estimation available?
If the "After Fall Asleep" mode or "Sleep Time Estimation" are missing:
*   **Hardware:** Both require phone hardware capable of background activity detection and a sensor batching queue of at least 500 events. If your device lacks this, the settings won't appear.
*   **Permissions:** Ensure **Motion & Fitness** (Activity Recognition) permissions are granted. Ensure background battery optimizations are disabled. Visit **[DontKillMyApp.com](https://dontkillmyapp.com/)**.

### 2. Why isn't automatic sleep tracking starting?
*   **Background Restrictions:** Ensure your phone isn't killing the app.
*   **Permissions:** Confirm **Physical Activity** permissions are granted.
*   **Charging:** If using **Sonar**, the phone must be plugged in for automatic start to trigger (by default). Check your preference in `Settings` ➔ `Sleep tracking` ➔ `Automatic sleep tracking`.

### 3. Why did tracking start by itself when I wasn't sleeping?
*   **Wearable Triggers:** Check if a smartwatch accidentally sent a "start tracking" command.
*   **Manual Mode:** If you prefer full manual control, set mode to **Manual Only**.
*   **Report a Bug:** If it repeatedly starts incorrectly, use `Left ☰ Menu` ➔ `Support` ➔ `Report a bug`.

### 4. Does automatic tracking drain a lot of battery?
No. Background activity monitoring uses low-power sensors and consumes very little battery. The app only switches to full tracking once it confirms you are asleep.
