# Red gaps in the sleep graph

Red gaps (or red vertical bars) on your sleep graph indicate periods where the app was unable to collect sensor data.

### 1. System Killing the App
If the red gaps are large (hours long), the Android system likely killed the app in the background to "optimize" memory or battery.
*   👉 **Fix:** You must exclude **Sleep as Android** from battery optimization. Visit **[DontKillMyApp.com](https://dontkillmyapp.com/)** for manufacturer-specific steps.

### 2. Wearable Connection Drops
If you track with a smartwatch, red gaps mean the Bluetooth connection between the watch and phone was lost.
*   👉 **Fix:**
    *   Ensure your **watch companion app** (e.g., Galaxy Wearable, Zepp, Garmin Connect) is also excluded from battery optimization.
    *   Keep your phone within Bluetooth range (usually the same room) throughout the night.

### 3. Sensor Issues
If using Sonar or the Accelerometer, physical movement or environmental noise might have interrupted the signal.
*   👉 **Fix:** Check your [Phone Placement](/general/start.html#phone_placement) and ensure the phone is on a stable surface.
