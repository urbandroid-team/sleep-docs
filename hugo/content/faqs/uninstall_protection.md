# I cannot uninstall the app

If the "Uninstall" button is disabled or greyed out in the Play Store or your system settings, it is because **Uninstall Protection** is active. This feature prevents accidental removal or "cheating" during an active alarm CAPTCHA.

### How to fix it:

*   **In-app Fix:** Open Sleep as Android and go to `Settings` ➔ `Alarms` ➔ `CAPTCHA` ➔ `CAPTCHA No Cheating` and turn off **Force stop and uninstall protection**.
*   **System Fix:** If you cannot open the app, go to your phone's `Settings` ➔ `Security` ➔ `Device Admin Apps` (the path may vary slightly by manufacturer) and revoke administrator privileges for **Sleep as Android**.

Once these steps are completed, you will be able to uninstall the app normally.
