# Goals

### 1. Do I lose my wager if I miss my bedtime once?
**No.** Missing a single night will not forfeit your goal. Goal success is based on your cumulative 30-day average, not daily perfection. As long as your overall balance (your "sleep bank") stays positive at the end of the goal period, you pass and keep your wager.

### 2. Where does my wager go?
Wagers are powered by Google Play in-app purchases. If you finish your goal successfully, the wager amount will be credited back to your Google Play account (or the purchase will be refunded). Placing a wager simply adds a financial incentive to help you form better sleep habits. You can always select the **Free** tier if you prefer not to bet.
