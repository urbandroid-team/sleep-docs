# Heart Rate (HR) & HRV issues

### 1. Why is Heart Rate not showing up on my sleep graph?
If your graph does not show the red HR line, check the following:
1.  **Wearable Configuration:** Verify that **Use wearable** is set to match your specific watch model in `Settings` ➔ `Sleep tracking` ➔ `Wearables`.
2.  **Sensor Lock:** Check if another health app on your phone or watch is maintaining an exclusive lock on the heart rate sensor, preventing Sleep as Android from accessing it.
3.  **System Settings:** If using Wear OS or a Galaxy Watch, ensure **continuous heart rate measurement** is enabled in your watch's system settings.

### 2. What is Heart Rate Variability (HRV) and why is higher usually better?
**HRV** measures the subtle time differences (in milliseconds) between consecutive heartbeats. A higher HRV indicates that your autonomic nervous system is responsive, balanced, and recovered. A sudden drop in your overnight HRV baseline can be an early signal of physical exhaustion, onset of illness, or high stress levels.

### 3. Does heart rate tracking drain my battery?
Continuous Bluetooth streaming of heart rate data consumes slightly more battery than standard motion tracking. If preserving battery is your priority, you can disable real-time HR streaming and instead let the app sync your heart rate data after you wake up via **Health Connect** or **Samsung Health**.
