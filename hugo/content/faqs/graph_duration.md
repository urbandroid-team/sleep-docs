# Graph duration issues

If your sleep tracking session started or ended at the wrong time, you can often correct the record manually.

### 1. The tracking started too early
If you started tracking before you actually got into bed, or if the app incorrectly detected sleep early:
*   👉 **Fix:** You can trim the beginning of the graph. Open the graph, tap the **pencil icon**, drag to select the excess period at the start, and tap the **scissors icon**.
*   See the [**Graph Editing guide**](/sleep/graph_edit.html#add_awake) for details.

### 2. The tracking started too late
If you forgot to start tracking or the automatic start triggered late:
*   **System Optimization:** Your phone's system may have prevented the app from starting on time to save battery.
    *   👉 **Fix:** Ensure **Sleep as Android** is excluded from all battery optimizations. Visit **[DontKillMyApp.com](https://dontkillmyapp.com/)** for manufacturer-specific instructions.
*   **False-positive Awakes:** The automatic start algorithm might have detected activity that it misclassified as you being awake, delaying the start of tracking.
    *   👉 **Fix:** Please contact our support team via **`Left ☰ Menu` → `Support` → `Report a bug`** so we can analyze your logs and fine-tune the detection for your device.
*   **Manual Correction:** You cannot add missing sensor data to an existing graph. However, you can add a manual sleep record for the missing period or create a new combined record via **Left ☰ Menu → Graphs → (+)**.

### 3. The tracking ended too early
If the app stopped tracking before you actually woke up:
*   **System Optimization:** Your phone's system may have killed the app in the background to save battery or free up memory.
    *   👉 **Fix:** Ensure **Sleep as Android** is excluded from all battery optimizations. Visit **[DontKillMyApp.com](https://dontkillmyapp.com/)** for manufacturer-specific instructions.
*   **False-positive Awakes:** If you use automatic tracking, the app might have detected activity that it misclassified as you being fully awake, causing it to terminate the session early.
    *   👉 **Fix:** Please contact our support team via **`Left ☰ Menu` → `Support` → `Report a bug`** so we can analyze your logs.
*   **Manual Correction:** You cannot recover missing sensor data. However, you can add a manual sleep record for the missing morning period using the **(+) Add sleep** button in the **Graphs** or **Statistics** screens.

### 4. The tracking ended too late
If you forgot to stop tracking in the morning:
*   👉 **Fix:** You can trim the end of the graph. Open the graph, tap the **pencil icon**, drag to select the excess period at the end, and tap the **scissors icon**.
*   See the [**Graph Editing guide**](/sleep/graph_edit.html#add_awake) for details.
