# Sleep score & Chart customization

### 1. Why is a specific score card or chart missing?
*   **Missing Sensors:** Your phone or wearable may lack the required sensor for that metric (e.g., no pulse oximeter for the SpO2/Disturbances chart).
*   **Gaps in Data:** If tracking was interrupted or battery optimization killed the app for part of the night, there might not be enough continuous data to calculate that specific score dimension.
*   **Settings:** Ensure the card isn't hidden under `Settings` ➔ `Personalize` ➔ `Morning screen`.

### 2. How can I change the order of the sleep score metrics?
If you want to prioritize the metrics that matter most to you, go to `Settings` ➔ `Personalize` ➔ `Stats` ➔ `Prioritize` and drag the metrics into your preferred display order.

### 3. Can I change the colors of the sleep score progress rings?
**Yes.** You can choose whether the gauges share your app-theme color or use unique color codes for each metric. Navigate to `Settings` ➔ `Personalize` ➔ `Stats` ➔ `Color palette` to select your preferred color scheme.
