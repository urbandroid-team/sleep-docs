# Blood Oxygen (SpO2) & Breathing issues

### 1. What should I do if my RDI score is high or concerning?
First, don't panic! A high score on a single night can be caused by temporary factors like a loose sensor, sleeping position, alcohol consumption, or nasal congestion.
1.  **Track for a few consecutive nights:** Verify if the high score is a recurring pattern or just an isolated event or sensor artifact.
2.  **Check your hardware setup:** Ensure your phone or wearable is positioned correctly and your oximeter strap isn't loose.
3.  **Try adjusting your sleep habits:** Sleeping on your back often increases breathing disturbances; try sleeping on your side.
4.  **Consult a medical professional:** If your RDI consistently remains above 15 (or if you experience symptoms like daytime fatigue, waking up gasping, or loud snoring), share your sleep graphs with a doctor or sleep specialist for a proper medical evaluation.

### 2. Why do I see temporary SpO2 disturbance dips?
Disruptions and sudden dips in the SpO2 graph can sometimes be caused by hardware factors - such as an oximeter slipping on your finger or a smartwatch losing skin contact during movement. Re-measuring over multiple nights helps filter out sensor artifacts from actual breathing issues.

### 3. What do the blue O2 bubbles on my graph mean?
An **O2 icon** ![O2 icon](/assets/icons/ic_action_cpap.svg) indicates a **desaturation event** - a temporary drop in blood oxygen levels (SpO2). Frequent drops contribute to your **[Respiratory Disturbance Index (RDI)](/sleep/breath_rate.html#rdi)**, which can be an early indicator of sleep apnea or other breathing issues.

### 4. Can Sleep as Android officially diagnose me with Sleep Apnea?
**No.** A formal medical diagnosis requires a clinical overnight study (polysomnography) supervised by professionals. Sleep as Android acts as an **early warning and screening tool** to alert you to potential patterns (like oxygen drops or breathing pauses) that you should discuss with a doctor.

### 5. What should I show my doctor if I suspect sleep issues?
You can share your sleep history with your healthcare provider via:
1.  **Online Read-Only Link (Recommended):** Generate a secure, read-only link via your [SleepCloud](/services/sleepcloud.html) account. This allows your doctor to inspect interactive graphs, breath rate trends, and noise recordings.
2.  **Screenshots:** Export or take screenshots of your sleep charts showing your **Breath Rate (RPM)**, **Snoring logs**, and **SpO2 graphs**.
