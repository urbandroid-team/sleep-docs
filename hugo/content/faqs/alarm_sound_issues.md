# Ringtone / Sound issues

### 1. Why didn't my selected ringtone play?
*   **File Issues:** Ensure local files weren't moved or deleted.
*   **Network/Radio:** If an online stream fails, the app plays a built-in fallback ringtone after 30 seconds.
*   **Spotify:** If the screen is locked, Spotify may resume your last session instead of switching playlists.

### 2. Why is my Silent Alarm playing a sound?
*   **Battery Optimization:** If vibration drivers are restricted, the app sounds a fallback ringtone to ensure you wake up. Disable battery optimizations.
*   **Direct Boot:** If your phone restarted and is at the lock screen, the app cannot access custom files and uses a built-in fallback sound.
