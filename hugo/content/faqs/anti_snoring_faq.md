# Anti-snoring issues

### 1. Why is Anti-snoring not working?
*   **Do Not Disturb (DND):** Ensure the app is in your DND exception list.
*   **Watch Settings:** Check that your watch isn't in a mode that blocks vibrations (Bedtime/Theater mode).
*   **Placement:** Ensure the phone's microphone isn't muffled by bedding.

### 2. Anti-snoring keeps waking me up completely
*   👉 **Fix:** Reduce the volume slider or lower the repetition frequency in `Settings` ➔ `Sleep noise analysis` ➔ `Anti-snoring`. Try using wearable vibration instead of audio cues.

### 3. Phone and watch both vibrate during preview, but only watch at night
**This is intentional.** The preview tests all hardware. During actual tracking, vibrations are routed exclusively to your wearable to keep alerts quiet and effective.
