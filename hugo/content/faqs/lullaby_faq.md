# Lullaby issues

### 1. Why does the lullaby volume drop soon after starting?
*   **"Turn Off When Sleeping" is active:** The app lowers media volume when it senses you're falling asleep. Go to `Settings` ➔ `Sleep tracking` ➔ `Lullabies` and increase the minimum playback duration.
*   **System DND / Sleep Mode:** Your phone's system settings might be muting media audio. Check your **Do Not Disturb** exceptions.

### 2. Can I use binaural beats through phone speakers?
**No.** Binaural beats work by sending slightly different frequencies to each ear, which requires stereo headphones or earbuds. Phone speakers mix these frequencies in the air, canceling out the neurological effect.

### 3. Why did my lullaby stop playing suddenly during the night?
*   **Battery Optimization:** Aggressive Android background battery management may terminate the app's background processes overnight.
*   Even when Sleep as Android recovers and resumes sleep tracking, the app intentionally does **not** restart lullaby audio automatically. This is by design to prevent sudden sound from startling or waking you while sleeping.
*   👉 **Fix:** Exclude **Sleep as Android** from battery optimization in your phone's system settings. Visit **[DontKillMyApp.com](https://dontkillmyapp.com/)** for step-by-step guides for your specific phone model.
