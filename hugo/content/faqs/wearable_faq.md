# Wearable connectivity & compatibility

### 1. My wearable disconnects during the night
If your sleep graph shows red gaps or if the connection to your watch is lost overnight, it is usually because Android is killing one of the necessary background components.

To maintain a stable connection, **all three** of the following must be excluded from battery optimization:
1.  **Sleep as Android**
2.  **Your watch's companion app** (e.g., Galaxy Wearable, Zepp, Garmin Connect, Pixel Watch app).
3.  **Bluetooth System App** (On some devices, you may need to show "System apps" in the battery settings to find this).

*   👉 **Fix:** Visit **[DontKillMyApp.com](https://dontkillmyapp.com/)** for specific instructions on how to disable optimizations for your phone manufacturer.

### 2. Which wearables can be used with Sleep as Android?
We support a wide range of devices including Wear OS (Pixel Watch, TicWatch, etc.), Garmin, Samsung Galaxy Watch, Mi Band, Amazfit, and more.

*   👉 **Fix:** You can find the full list of integrated devices and their supported sensors in our [**Supported Wearables List**](/devices/supported_wearable.html).
