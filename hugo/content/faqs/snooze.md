# Snooze issues

If the Snooze button is missing or the snooze duration doesn't match your settings, please check the following troubleshooting steps.

### 1. Why can't I see the Snooze button?

If the Snooze button is missing when your alarm rings, leaving only the **Dismiss** option, it is due to one of the following safety limits:

*   **Snooze Limit or Maximum Total Snooze Time:** You may have reached your pre-set snooze limit (number of snoozes) or the maximum allowed total snooze time. Check your settings in `Settings` ➔ `Alarms` ➔ `Snooze` (or in the specific alarm's settings).
*   **Snooze After Alarm Time:** If you are using **Smart Wake Up** and the alarm triggered before your scheduled time, snoozing might be disabled once your original alarm time is reached. Check if **Snooze after alarm time** is disabled in the Snooze settings.

### 2. Snooze time doesn't match my settings

If the snooze duration shown on your alarm screen is different from what you configured, check for these active features:

*   **Halve the Snooze:** This feature cut each consecutive snooze duration in half (e.g., 20 min → 10 min → 5 min).
    *   👉 **Fix:** Disable **Halve the snooze** in `Settings` ➔ `Alarms` ➔ `Snooze`.
*   **Maximum Total Snooze Time:** If your remaining allowed total snooze time is shorter than your default snooze duration, the button will automatically adjust to fit only the time you have left.
    *   👉 **Fix:** Increase or disable the **Maximum total snooze time** limit.
*   **Per-Alarm Overrides:** You might have customized snooze options for a specific alarm that differ from your global defaults.
    *   👉 **Fix:** Check the individual **Alarm-specific settings** for the alarm in question.
