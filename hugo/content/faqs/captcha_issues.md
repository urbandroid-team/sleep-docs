# CAPTCHA issues

### 1. Why didn't my QR Code scanner work?
*   **Reason:** Third-party scanners cannot run while **Power Off Protection** or **Uninstall Protection** is active.
*   👉 **Fix:** Try switching to a native scanner like Google Lens or Barcode Scanner. Reset scanner defaults in `System Settings` ➔ `Apps` ➔ `Sleep` ➔ `Clear Defaults`. Test the scanner in the `Preview` menu.

### 2. Why did CAPTCHA trigger when I was away from home?
*   **Reason:** The **"Only at Home"** feature relies on broad location checks (2.5–5 km). It requires a history of at least 5 recorded sleep graphs at your home location to establish a baseline.
*   👉 **Fix:** Ensure you have enough home sleep history before relying on this feature.

### 3. Can I set different tasks for different alarms?
**Yes.** You can choose different tasks for each individual alarm (e.g., harder tasks for workdays). Go to the individual `Alarm settings` ➔ `CAPTCHA` and select your preferred task.
