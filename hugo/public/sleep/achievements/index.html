    Achievements#

    Sleep Achievements are reward badges that celebrate your healthy sleep habits, track personal milestones, and help you discover everything Sleep as Android has to offer.

    Whether you are looking to build a consistent bedtime routine or simply want an easy way to explore the app’s rich features, achievements offer a fun, optional way to track your journey.

    What Are Achievements?#

    Achievements are digital milestone badges unlocked as you use the app. They serve two main purposes:

    1. Building Healthy Habits: Recognize your progress when maintaining consistent bedtimes, reaching sleep goals, or tracking continuously.
    2. Exploring Features: Learn about helpful features—like CAPTCHA alarms, lullabies, sonar tracking, or sleep noise analysis—as you naturally use them.

    💡 Your Sleep, Your Choice Achievements are completely optional! If you prefer a streamlined, distraction-free dashboard, you can easily dismiss or hide the Achievements card at any time.

    How to Access & Track Your Achievements#

    View Achievements on the Dashboard#

    • Open Sleep as Android.
    • Scroll to the Achievements card on your main dashboard to see your latest unlocked badges and upcoming milestones.

    View Full Collection#

    • Tap the Achievements card or go to Left ☰ menu → Achievements.
    • Browse through:
      • Unlocked Badges: Achievements you have completed, along with the date unlocked.
      • In Progress / Locked Badges: Hints and criteria for badges you can unlock next.

    Key Achievement Categories#

    Category Description Examples
    Getting Started Basic onboarding and first steps in sleep tracking. First Good Night, First Lullaby, Smart Wakeup Debut
    Consistency & Routine Building healthy, repeatable sleep schedules over time. Night Owl No More, Weekend Rested, 7-Day Streak
    Feature Exploration Trying out advanced tracking, smart alarms, and relaxation tools. Mindful Sleep, CAPTCHA Champion, Sound Listener
    Milestones Long-term tracking accomplishments. 100 Nights Tracked, Year of Better Sleep

    Sharing Your Progress#

    Celebrating your sleep improvement can keep you motivated:

    • Tap on any unlocked badge to see its details.
    • Select the Share icon to send your badge to friends, family, or social media.

    ❓ FAQs & Troubleshooting#

    Can I hide or disable Achievements? Yes! If you do not want to see Achievements on your dashboard: - Simply **swipe left** on the Achievements card to dismiss it. - Alternatively, scroll to the bottom, tap the Hide / Show button and untick the Achievement option. ize → Dashboard** and uncheck the **Achievements** card.
    I can't find Achievements on the main screen? If the Achievement card is not visible, scroll down to the bottom, tap the Hide / Show button and check the **Achievements** card.
    Do Achievements turn sleep into a competition? Not at all. Achievements are strictly personal milestones designed to guide you through app features and encourage positive habits. Sleep recovery is individual, so focus only on what works best for your health.

    Need further help? Contact us via Left ☰ Menu → Support → Report a bug.
