    Advice

      • General Description
      • Watched Dimensions
      • Types of Advice Cards
      • Examples

    Advice shows your current sleep development at a glance.

    You can see which dimensions of your sleep have improved or worsened recently, including tips on how to reverse worsening trends.

    Menu Path: Left ☰ Menu → Advice (or via the Advice dashboard card)

    General Description#

    Advice monitors recent trends in critical sleep dimensions (Duration, Regularity, Deep sleep %, Snoring, Social Jet-lag, etc.).

    In most cases, it compares your last 60 days of aggregate data to the last 7 days to identify significant recent changes. It also checks whether your data falls within healthy bounds established by public health studies.

    Watched Dimensions#

    DimensionChecks
    Duration
    • Recent improvement or worsening (last 7 days vs. last 60 days).
    • Healthy bounds for your age: General (6.5–9h), School age (9–11h), Teenager (8–10h), Young adult (7–9h), Elderly (6–9h).
    • Consistency with your Daily Sleep Duration Goal.
    • Comparison to average data in your country.
    Regularity
    • Recent improvement or worsening.
    • Healthy bounds for the 60-day average (ideally under 20 minutes variance).
    • National comparisons.
    Regularity Index
    • 5-point shift in the last 7 days compared to the previous month.
    • Healthy (over 80) vs. unhealthy (under 60) benchmarks.
    Deep Sleep %
    • Recent shifts in deep sleep ratios.
    • Healthy bounds for the 60-day average (typically 25% – 75%).
    Snoring
    • Frequency shifts.
    • Warnings for sessions exceeding 30 or 60 minutes.
    • National comparisons.
    Social Jet-lag
    • 60-day average check (ideally under 1 hour variance between work and off days).

    You can configure your age for more accurate duration benchmarks in Settings → Sleep → Stats → Year of birth.

    Types of Advice Cards#

    • Congrats: Highlights a positive improvement in a specific sleep dimension.
    • Caution: Identifies worsening trends with tips on how to improve and links to set up Sleep Goals.
    • Neutral: Provides contextual data that isn’t strictly positive or negative (e.g., comparing your duration to your country’s average).

    Examples#

    • Congrats: Your SNORING is down by 5 minutes lately.
    • Caution: Your DURATION is down by 30 minutes lately.
    • Neutral: Others in your country are getting 54 minutes more DURATION.
    • Caution: Your SNORING is over 50 minutes.
    • Congrats: Your IRREGULARITY is under 20 minutes.
