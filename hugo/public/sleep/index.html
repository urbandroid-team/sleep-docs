    Sleep Tracking#

    Sleep as Android uses advanced algorithms and your device’s sensors to provide a detailed window into your nightly rest. By understanding your sleep cycles, you can make informed decisions to improve your health, productivity, and overall well-being.

    Use this section to learn about the science behind our tracking, how to interpret your results, and how to use our advanced monitoring tools.

    Foundations#

    Understand the technology and get started with tracking.

    • Sleep Tracking Theory: Learn about sleep phases (Deep, Light, REM) and how actigraphy works.
    • How to Track Sleep: A step-by-step guide to starting your first session.
    • Sensors Overview: Explore the different ways the app can “see” your movement (Accelerometer, Sonar, Wearables).

    Analysis & Results#

    Learn how to read the data the app collects each night.

    • How to Read Sleep Graphs: A comprehensive guide to understanding your nightly hypnogram.
    • Sleep Score: Discover how we calculate your overall sleep health percentage.
    • Statistics & Trends: Analyze your progress over weeks, months, and years.
    • Charts & Trends: Deep dive into specific metrics like regularity and deep sleep percentage.

    Sleep Quality & Health#

    Monitor vitals and identify potential sleep-related health issues.

    • Breath Rate & Oxygen (SpO2): Track your respiratory health during the night.
    • Sleep Apnea Pre-screening: Use the app to identify potential breathing disturbances.
    • Heart Rate Monitoring: Integrate pulse data from your wearable.
    • Anti-snoring: Use gentle cues to help you or your partner stop snoring.
    • Chronotype & Regularity: Find out if you’re a “Morning Lark” or “Night Owl.”

    Advanced Monitoring Tools#

    Use AI and automated features to capture every detail.

    • Sound Recognition: Automatically identify snoring, talking, coughing, or baby cries.
    • Sleep Noise Analysis: Record and listen back to significant nightly sounds.
    • Automatic Tracking: Set the app to start tracking automatically when you go to bed.
    • Awake Detection: Learn how the app identifies when you’re no longer asleep.
    • Pair Tracking: Filter out your partner’s movement for a cleaner graph.

    Improvement & Goals#

    Turn your sleep data into better habits.

    • Sleep Advice: Get personalized tips based on your recent sleep trends.
    • Sleep Goals: Set targets for duration, regularity, or deep sleep.
    • Lullabies: Fall asleep faster with nature sounds, white noise, and binaural beats.
    • Lucid Dreaming: Use REM-triggered cues to master your dreams.
    • Jet Lag Prevention: Use light therapy and schedule shifts to combat travel fatigue.
