    Breath rate detection & blood oxygen measurement

    Monitor your breathing and blood oxygen saturation to detect respiratory issues like sleep apnea.

    Sleep as Android uses advanced sensors to track these metrics:

    • Breath Rate: Measured via Sonar, Sleep Phaser, or Polar H10.
    • Blood Oxygen (SpO2): Measured via a standalone Bluetooth Oximeter or compatible smartwatch.

    Visualizing Data in the Graph#

    • Turquoise Line: Represents your Breath Rate (breaths per minute).
    • Blue Line: Represents your SpO2 (oxygen saturation percentage).
    • Breathing Disturbance: Marked by the O2 icon. This appears whenever a significant dip in oxygen or breath rate is detected.

    SpO2 data on the sleep graph

    Respiratory Disturbance Index (RDI) #

    The RDI is the average number of breathing disturbance episodes detected per hour. It is a critical indicator of sleep quality and potential health issues.

    RDI ScoreClassificationAction Recommended
    0 – 10NormalLikely caused by minor movements or sensor fit.
    10 – 15MildRepeat measurements to ensure accuracy.
    15 – 30ModerateA potential warning sign. Verify with repeated tracks.
    30+SevereWe strongly recommend consulting a doctor.

    Tracking Methods#

    Sonar & Sleep Phaser#

    Both Sonar and Sleep Phaser use ultrasonic or infrared waves to detect the subtle movement of your chest and abdomen. No extra setup is required; if the signal is strong enough, the data appears automatically on your graph.

    Pulse Oximeters#

    Standalone oximeters provide the most precise SpO2 tracking. They are highly recommended if you suspect Sleep Apnea.

    • Path: Settings → Sleep tracking → Wearables → Pulse oximeter (Bluetooth)

    Compatible Smartwatches#

    Select Wear OS and Garmin wearables can also provide SpO2 data.

    • Path: Settings → Sleep tracking → Wearables → Pulse oximeter (Wear OS, Garmin..)

    Not all wearables support third-party SpO2 streaming. Check the compatibility table for details.

    Low Breath Rate Alarm#

    This safety feature wakes you up if your breathing rate or oxygen saturation remains dangerously low for a sustained period. This encourages you to change positions and resume normal breathing.

    Menu Path: Settings → Sleep tracking → Wearables → Low breath rate alarm

    This alert is designed to be very loud and prominent for your safety. Use with caution.
