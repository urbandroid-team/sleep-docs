    Add-ons

    Enhance your Sleep as Android experience with these official add-ons and compatible services:

    • SleepCloud: Cloud backup and synchronization.
    • Sleep Phaser: Contactless smart bedside lamp.
    • Wearables: Smartwatch and fitness tracker integrations.
    • Lullabies: Extra high-quality soundscapes.
    • Oximeter: Continuous SpO2 monitoring.
    • CAPTCHA Pack: More puzzles to wake you up.
    • Sleep Mask: Light stimulation for sleep and lucid dreaming.
    • Mindroid: Binaural beat stimulation.
