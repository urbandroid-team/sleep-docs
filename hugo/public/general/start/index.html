    Welcome to Sleep as Android! This guide will help you understand the benefits of sleep tracking and how to get the most out of your new sleep assistant.

    Why Track Your Sleep?#

    Sleep is a fundamental pillar of health. Consistent monitoring helps you understand how your lifestyle choices (like caffeine, exercise, or late-night screen time) impact your rest.

    The two main benefits are:

    1. Insight: Identify long-term patterns and improve your sleep habits over time.
    2. Smart Wake-up: Our signature feature identifies when you are in a light sleep phase and wakes you up gently, preventing that “groggy” feeling in the morning.

    What Does the App Measure?#

    To build a 360-degree map of your night, Sleep as Android analyzes data from several sources:

    • Movement: Uses your phone’s accelerometer or Sonar to estimate sleep phases via actigraphy.
    • Vitals: Collects heart rate (HR), variability (HRV), and blood oxygen (SpO2) from compatible wearables or oximeters.
    • Environment: Monitors ambient light and noise levels (snoring, talking, or disturbances).
    • Lifestyle: Uses your custom #tags to correlate daily activities with nightly results.

    Your First Steps#

    1. Set Your First Alarm#

    Create an alarm and experience Smart Wake-up. The app will look for the best moment to wake you up within a set window before your alarm time.

    Learn how to create an alarm here.

    2. Start Your First Tracking Session#

    Place your phone on your mattress or connect your wearable.

    Read our guide on starting tracking.

    3. Review Your Results#

    In the morning, explore your Sleep Graph to see your sleep cycles and check your Sleep Score to see how you performed across different dimensions.

    Advanced Features to Explore#

    • CAPTCHAs: Stop oversleeping by requiring a task to be solved before the alarm turns off.
    • Lullabies: Fall asleep faster with natural soundscapes or white noise.
    • Automatic Tracking: Let the app start tracking for you so you never forget a night.
    • Cloud Backup: Keep your data safe and synced across devices with SleepCloud.
