    Sleep mask#

    Enhance your sleep using light stimulation. The Sleep Mask helps you fall asleep faster, wake up naturally, and master lucid dreaming.

    Designed specifically for use with Sleep as Android and Mindroid.

    • Sunrise Alarm: Gently transitions from low red to full yellow, waking you up without jarring sounds.
    • Master Your Dreams: Use light cues to trigger and remember Lucid Dreaming.
    • Anti-snoring: Provides gentle visual cues to encourage position shifts.
    • Fast Fall-Asleep: Use light-stimulated lullabies and Mindroid programs.

    Get the Mask: You can purchase the mask from Happy Electronics. For more technical details, visit our dedicated Sleep Mask website.

    Mask Types#

    Left: Original wired version. Right: V2 Wireless (Bluetooth) version.

    Setup Guide#

    Option A: Wired (Cable) Mask#

    1. Enable Smartlight: In Sleep as Android, go to Settings → Services → Smartlight and ensure the master switch at the top is Enabled.
    2. Connect Hardware: Plug the mask into your phone’s micro-USB or USB-C port using the provided adapter. A small diode will light up to confirm power.
    3. Activate in App: Scroll down to the Sleep Mask section and check Use SleepMask.

    Option B: Wireless (Bluetooth) Mask V2#

    1. Power On: Turn on the mask using its physical switch.
    2. Permissions: Ensure Bluetooth and Location are enabled on your phone.
    3. Enable Smartlight: In Sleep as Android, go to Settings → Services → Smartlight and ensure the master switch is Enabled.
    4. Activate: Check the Use SleepMask box.
    5. Pair: Tap Connect Sleep Mask V2, select your mask from the list, and wait for the confirmation blink.

    All features (Sunrise Alarm, Anti-Snoring, Lucid Dreaming) are enabled automatically once the mask is active.

    The app cannot maintain simultaneous Bluetooth connections to both a Sleep Mask V2 and a Sleep Phaser.

    Need further help? Contact us via Left ☰ Menu → Support → Report a bug.
